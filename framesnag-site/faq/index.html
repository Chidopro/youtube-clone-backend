FrameSnag FAQ

1. Why isn’t my screenshot downloading?

Agree to the Terms of Use in the extension and select a resolution. Downloads stay disabled until those steps are done.

2. The extension popup doesn’t open or closes quickly

Refresh the YouTube tab. Avoid Incognito unless FrameSnag is allowed there. Use Chrome, Edge, Brave, or another Chromium browser.

3. What resolution are screenshots?

Up to Full HD quality, depending on the source video.

4. How does FrameSnag work with ScreenMerch?

Approved ScreenMerch creators can capture frames from their own videos and send them into Dashboard → Pages for merchandise artwork.

5. Is FrameSnag free?

Yes. Install from the Chrome Web Store at no cost.
