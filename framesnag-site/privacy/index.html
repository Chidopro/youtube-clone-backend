Privacy Policy

FrameSnag is a Chrome extension and website operated in connection with ScreenMerch.

What we collect

The marketing website may collect basic server logs (such as IP address and pages visited) through our hosting provider. Support emails you send to support@framesnag.com include whatever you choose to write.

The extension

FrameSnag captures frames from YouTube pages you open for your own use and ScreenMerch workflows. It is not intended to sell personal data. Review Chrome Web Store permissions before installing.

ScreenMerch
